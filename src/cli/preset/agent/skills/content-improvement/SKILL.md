# Content Improvement

Research the target page exactly as described by [Executor Starters](references/executor.md). During
the research turn, diagnose intent, substance, differentiation, support, and sibling-page ownership
without editing files.

During the host-authorized action turn, submit edits only for recommendations accepted by the
decision report. Preserve the repository's public positioning and voice, target the owning source
rather than generated output, and keep the change local to the selected page and intent. Record a
review item instead of an edit when the owning source or supporting evidence is unclear. Do not invent metrics, customers,
capabilities, or citations.

PageGraph leaves applied edits uncommitted for review. Provider state remains read-only.
